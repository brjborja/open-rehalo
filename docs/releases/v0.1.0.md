The first public release of rehalo, an independent, open-source iPhone app for the SmartHalo 2. It's an
early beta: it has been tested with a real ring, but not yet on many rides.

## What it does

- **Navigation:** import a GPX route or search for a place, and get turn guidance on the ring.
  Destinations follow Apple's route and its turns, with a City, Road or Trails profile. Compass mode
  points at your destination. The screen shows the same turns as the ring. Includes a Live Activity
  and a 3D route view.
- **Alarm:** arms when you leave and disarms when your iPhone returns, with a five-tap code on the ring.
- **Ring cards and shortcuts:** clock, battery, speed, distance, ride time and average speed, plus tap
  shortcuts for the horn, the light and quick readings.
- **Front light:** on/off, brightness, steady or blinking.
- **Call and rain alerts** on the ring. Rain alerts are optional and use
  [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/).
- **Ring recovery:** reclaim a ring whose password was lost.
- **Demo mode:** explore the app without a ring.

## Install

Download `rehalo-unsigned.ipa` below and sideload it with your own Apple ID, using
[iLoader](https://github.com/nab138/iloader) over USB or [SideStore](https://sidestore.io). Needs
iOS 17 or later and a real iPhone. The [README](https://github.com/cyberlynx33/open-rehalo#install-the-ios-app)
has the details.

## Known limits

- No firmware updates over the air, online rerouting, app notifications on the ring, or Strava and
  Apple Health sync.
- Route profiles, Apple's alternative routes and roundabouts haven't been checked on a ride yet.
- The ring only draws the off-route pointer while the bike is moving.
