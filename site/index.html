A new app for your SmartHalo 2. No account or servers needed.

rehalo is an independent, open-source app that lets you use the ring on your handlebars again, without the original app. It's on iPhone today, and an Android app is planned.

What happened

  1. November 2021

    The company behind the ring ran out of cash and closed. The servers the devices relied on went with it.

  2. What was left

    The front light and the alarm still worked. The rest needed the official app, and the app needed those servers.

  3. Afterwards

    The firmware was published so a community could keep the devices going. People documented the API and took the ring apart. rehalo builds on that work.

  4. October 2026

    rehalo started from that source code. The app was rebuilt around the ring's Bluetooth protocol, and it doesn't depend on any server that could shut down.

What's back

Navigation, alarm, ride stats and alerts.

Every animation below is the ring's own. They're ported from the firmware source and checked against it frame by frame. Long stretches of a ride are shortened here.

Turn-by-turn on the ring

Load a GPX route or search for a place. Near each turn an arc lights up on the side you'll turn, fills green from its centre as you get closer and blinks when it's time. The small display shows the street, and the whole ring pulses green when you arrive.

Go off route and an orange pointer leads you back.

Back

Or just point me there

Compass mode skips the route. A pointer holds the direction of your destination and shifts from orange to green as you get closer. You pick the streets.

Back

It watches your bike

Walk away and the Halo arms itself, then goes dark. Move the bike and orange arcs warn you off, then the alarm sounds and the ring strobes. Come back with your iPhone and it stands down. If your phone is dead, tap your five-beat code on the ring.

The app tells you how many times the alarm went off while you were away.

Back

Ride stats and tap shortcuts

Clock, battery, speed, distance, ride time and average speed, one swipe apart on the ring. Short and long taps trigger the horn, the light or a quick reading.

Back

A call, without reaching for your phone

The whole ring pulses blue and the display shows the call. One short tap silences it.

iOS doesn't share the caller's name with apps, so the ring can't show it. It works while you ride or navigate.

New

Rain warning

The firmware has a storm icon that the official app never used. If rain is due within half an hour, the ring now pulses a deeper blue and shows that icon for about six seconds. It warns you once, and waits if you're about to turn.

Off by default. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo, with no key or account.

Good to know

Which devices does it work with?

The SmartHalo 2 and an iPhone on iOS 17 or later. An Android app is planned. The first-generation SmartHalo is a different design and isn't supported.

How do I install it?

rehalo isn't on the App Store yet. For now it's installed by sideloading with your own Apple ID, using a tool such as SideStore.

Can I reclaim a ring that was paired to the old app?

Yes, that's what the recovery wizard is for. A quirk of the firmware means the ring has to reboot once, and if its battery won't run down that can mean opening the case to disconnect it.
